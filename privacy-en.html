Privacy Policy

Image Dimension Filter

繁體中文

Effective date: September 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Image Dimension Filter does not collect, receive, or transmit to the developer any personally identifiable information, usage data, or analytics. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking.

2. Local Processing

All image processing, resolution filtering, and file management take place entirely on your device. No images, file paths, or metadata are uploaded to the developer, to cloud services, or to any third party.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To work properly, the app stores the following only on your own computer (in your user profile folder and the Windows registry): your settings (such as filter preferences and language), your list of locked files, an error log (technical error messages, which may include file paths), and a trial usage record (a count and fingerprints of counted images — never the images themselves). This data never leaves your computer unless you choose to send it, for example by emailing the error log to us for support. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete this data; contact us if you need help removing it.

4. Microsoft Store

The app uses Windows' built-in Microsoft Store services to check whether you have purchased the full version and to process purchases. This is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The developer does not receive your payment details or account information.

5. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please email: imgdf.service@gmail.com
